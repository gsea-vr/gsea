Dive in
−10m Surface waters

Open water, wide and quiet

Drop in and the surface light is still with you. Currents push gently at your gear, and the only sound is your own breathing as you get your bearings before heading down.

−45m The drop-off

Something is circling

The light thins out and the reef falls away into open blue. This is where sharks start to show up on the edge of visibility — close enough to feel watched, far enough that you can't be sure.

−90m Pearl beds

Progress lives down here

Pearls cluster where the current pools against old rock. Collecting them is how you gear up and unlock new parts of the map — but every trip down means a longer, more exposed trip back up.

−160m The trench

Where the horror starts

Past a certain depth the ocean stops feeling explorable and starts feeling inhabited. Multiplayer runs down here rely on sticking together — visibility is low, and not everything down there is a shark.

About G-Sea

G-Sea is an underwater VR game built around exploration. Dive through open water, encounter sharks, and work your way through progression, gear, and challenges as you collect Pearls and uncover different parts of the ocean.
